ICASSP 2027 – Tanver 
Central idea: Transferring from human speech foundation models to animal acoustics for identification. 
 Sub questions: 
Do multilingual speech foundation models produce more discriminative animal-vocalization representations than comparable monolingual speech models when the encoders are frozen? 
How does supervised fine-tuning on animal vocalizations affect the performance gap between monolingual and multilingual speech foundation models? 
Does multilingual pretraining provide a larger advantage when only a small amount of labeled animal data is available? 
Which acoustic characteristics—such as frequency range, recording noise, call duration, or taxonomic group—predict successful transfer from speech to animal vocalizations?
